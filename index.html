Ask your own data

Which accounts have an open support ticket and renew in the next 30 days?

Nine accounts, holding $14,200 of monthly recurring revenue.

SELECT a.name, count(t.ticket_id), s.current_period_end
FROM accounts a
JOIN subscriptions s ON s.account_id = a.account_id
LEFT JOIN support_tickets t ON t.account_ref = a.name

accounts v14, subscriptions v9, support_tickets v3
